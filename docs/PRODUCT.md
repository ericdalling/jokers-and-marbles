# Product vision

A premium shared-screen Jokers & Marbles game for Apple TV, with private hands and controls on iPhone and iPad. Warm wooden board, glass marbles, readable player labels, turn indicator, public discard and animated moves. No private hands on TV.

First milestone: six local players, two teams of three or three teams of two (team seating and rules to be confirmed). Join via local discovery and room code. No account or internet required.

The approved visual concept is aesthetic guidance, not an authoritative board topology. Confirm five-marble rules, card effects, team seating, deck count and win conditions before implementing rules.
